Tutorial Stage

AI powered gaming controls assistant

About The App

Tutorial Stage is an app dedicated to helping gamers learn to control their favorite games, and get familiar with their objectives. The app provides an easy AI base interface, where users can input select details about a video game, and the app will give you the controlling setup, and the basic gameplay. The app can also recommend video games for the user to play, cementing itself as an essential asset in a gamer’s vast toolkit.

🎮 Game Controls

Learn keyboard, controller, and gameplay controls quickly and easily for video games of your choice, on a multitude of gaming devices.

🧠 AI Assistance

Get AI-powered explanations for game mechanics and objectives, based on your needs.

🔥 Game Recommendations

Discover interesting games, with the video game spotlight feature. This allows users to get recomended an excellent game to try out.

Privacy Policy

Tutorial Stage does not collect or share personal user data. Any information entered into the app is only used to provide gameplay guidance and recommendations.
